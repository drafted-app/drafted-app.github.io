How it works

They sign up

Send your sign-up link or put it in your bio. Clients answer about two minutes of questions: body, goal, training days, foods they like and won't eat.

The plan is built

Training, meals, calories and macros for every phase. Allergies and diet are respected, and portions are sized to hit the numbers.

You check and publish

Change any exercise, swap any meal or set your own calories. Nothing reaches the client until you press publish.

You coach

They log workouts, meals, weigh-ins and check-ins in the app. Your dashboard sorts out who needs you today.

A look inside

One form, whole plan

Fill it in yourself or let the client do it through your sign-up link.

  • Lose fat or build muscle, 12 or 16 weeks
  • 3 to 5 days, gym or home
  • Everyday, allergy-friendly or vegetarian menus
  • Likes, dislikes and foods to include
  • Your own calories if you'd rather set them

Every client on one page

Their weight against where the plan expects them to be, every check-in, recent training and food, and the full plan. Change the plan from here, and a "what changed" note goes to the client with it.

Your app, your name

Clients get a phone app with your brand, colour and logo. It covers today's workout, meals, weigh-ins, check-ins and reminders. They add it to their home screen, and there's nothing to download from an app store.

Set your look once

Your brand name, any colour, and your logo, which becomes the app icon on their phone. Nothing in your clients' app mentions Drafted.

Questions coaches ask

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. If you can fill in a form and copy a link, you can run it. We set your account up and send you a link to choose your password.

Can I change what it builds?

Yes. Any exercise, any meal and the calories. Nothing reaches the client until you publish.

Will my clients see your name?

No. They see your brand, your colour and your logo.

Who can see my clients?

Only you. Each coach has their own login, and one coach can never see another's clients.

Does it take payments from my clients?

Not yet. Keep charging clients the way you do now.

Who is it for?

Online and hybrid coaches working with adults on fat loss and muscle gain. Plans are built for clients 18 and over.

What if I go over my client limit?

You'll see a note when you try to add one more client. We move you to the next tier the same day.
